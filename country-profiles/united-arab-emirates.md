# United Arab Emirates transfer pricing rules

Transfer pricing rules in United Arab Emirates, summarised from the OECD Transfer Pricing Country Profile for United Arab Emirates (updated October 2025). ## The Arm's Length Principle

### Q1. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework make reference to the arm's length principle?

**Yes.**

Sources: [Federal Tax Authority Transfer Pricing Guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2023/Transfer%20Pricing%20Guide%20-%20EN%20-%2023%2010%202023.pdf)

### Q2. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework give the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines any role or status?

**Yes.**

There is currently no explicit reference to the OECD Guidelines in UAE Corporate Tax Legislation, but UAE transfer pricing guidance does refer to the 2022 Transfer Pricing Guidelines which acts a san interpretation of the arm's length standard for UAE Corporate Tax purposes.

Sources: [Federal Tax Authority Transfer Pricing Guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2023/Transfer%20Pricing%20Guide%20-%20EN%20-%2023%2010%202023.pdf)

### Q3. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for a definition of related parties applicable for transfer pricing purposes?

**Yes.**

Related Parties are defined in Article 35 of the UAE Corporate Tax Law. Related parties are defined as any of the following: a) Two or more natural persons who are related within the fourth degree of kinship or affiliation, including by way of adoption or guardianship. b) A natural person and a juridical person where:

1. the natural person or one or more Related Parties of the natural person are shareholders in the juridical person, and the natural person, alone or together with its Related Parties, directly or indirectly owns a 50% (fifty percent) or greater ownership interest in the juridical person; or 2. the natural person, alone or together with its Related Parties, directly or indirectly Controls the juridical person. c) Two or more juridical persons where: 1. one juridical person, alone or together with its Related Parties, directly or indirectly owns a 50% (fifty percent) or greater ownership interest in the other juridical person;

2. one juridical person, alone or together with its Related Parties, directly or indirectly Controls the other juridical person; or 3. any Person, alone or together with its Related Parties, directly or indirectly owns a 50% (fifty percent) or greater ownership interest in or Controls such two or more juridical persons; d) A Person and its Permanent Establishment or Foreign Permanent Establishment e) Two or more Persons that are partners in the same Unincorporated Partnership f) A Person who is the trustee, founder, settlor or beneficiary of a trust or foundation

"Control" means the ability of a Person, whether in their own right or by agreement or otherwise to influence another Person, including: a) The ability to exercise 50% (fifty percent) or more of the voting rights of another Person. b) The ability to determine the composition of 50% (fifty percent) or more of the Board of directors of another Person. c) The ability to receive 50% (fifty percent) or more of the profits of another Person. d) The ability to determine, or exercise significant influence over, the conduct of the Business and affairs of another Person.

Sources: [UAE Corporate Tax Law Federal Decree Law No. 47](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-EN.pdf)

## Transfer Pricing Methods

### Q4. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for transfer pricing methods to be used in respect of transactions between related parties?

**Yes.**

Methods provided for: CUP, Resale Price, Cost Plus, TNMM, Profit Split, Other.

Any Other Method is permitted to be used if the taxpayer can demonstrate that none of the five OECD recognised methods (i.e. CUP, RPM, Cost Plus, TNMM and Profit Split) can be reasonably applied to determine an arm's length result and that it provides for the same result that would have occurred had the transaction occurred between independent parties.

Sources: [UAE Corporate Tax Law Federal Decree Law No. 47](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-EN.pdf)

### Q5. Which criterion is provided for in your domestic transfer pricing framework for the application of transfer pricing methods?

Most appropriate method.

### Q6. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework contain specific guidance on commodity transactions?

**Yes.**

For controlled transactions involving commodities, the guidance contained in paragraphs 2.18-2.22 of the TPG is followed.

## Transfer Pricing Documentation

### Q29. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework require the taxpayer to prepare transfer pricing documentation?

**Yes.**

Required documentation: Master file consistent with Annex I to Chapter V of the TPG; Local file consistent with Annex II to Chapter V of the TPG; Country-by-country report consistent with Annex III to Chapter V of the TPG; Specific transfer pricing returns (separate or annexed to the tax return).

Sources: [Ministerial Decision No. 97 of 2023 Requirements for Maintaining Transfer Pricing Documentation](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Ministerial-Decision-No.-97-of-2023-for-the-Purposes-of-Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022.pdf); [Federal Tax Authority Transfer Pricing Guide](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Pdf/2023/Transfer%20Pricing%20Guide%20-%20EN%20-%2023%2010%202023.pdf)

### Q30. Please briefly explain the relevant requirements related to each transfer pricing documentation requirement.

The Master File and Local File are not required to be submitted but may be requested by the Federal Tax Authority, in which case such documentation should be provided within 30 days. The Files may be in English and/or Arabic.

Broadly speaking, taxpayers must maintain both Master File and Local file if they either are a) a constituent entity of a MNE that has total consolidated group revenues of 3.15 billion UAE Dirhams (AED) in a Tax Period; or b) the taxpayer has revenue in a Tax period of AED 200 million or more.

The CbC report must be prepared by all MNE groups headquartered in the UAE that have consolidated group revenue in the preceding year equal to or above AED 3.15 billion (approx. EUR 750 million). The Ultimate Parent Entity will be required to file the CbC report no later than 12 months after the last day of each reporting year of the MNE Group in the UAE.

The Corporate Tax Return also requires taxpayers to file a TP disclosure form disclosing all transactions with related parties (as defined in Article 35 of the UAE Corporate Tax Law) where the aggregate amount exceeds AED 40 million. Separately, payments to connected persons (as defined under Article 36) must also be disclosed, as part of the disclosure, if the aggregate payment/benefit exceeds AED 500 000.

### Q31. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for specific transfer pricing penalties and/or compliance incentives?

**No.**

There are no specific transfer pricing penalties in the UAE tax legislation. However, the applicable Corporate Tax penalties (outlined in Cabinet Decision No. 75 of 2023) would apply such that, for example, penalties for not maintaining appropriate records and information would be applicable for not maintaining appropriate transfer pricing records for related party transactions.

### Q32. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for exemption from transfer pricing documentation obligations?

**Yes.**

Taxpayers that fall below the thresholds above are not required to maintain a Master File or Local File but must maintain records to support the arm's length nature of its related party transactions or payments to Connected Persons.

## Dispute Avoidance and Resolution

### Q33. Which mechanisms are available in your jurisdiction to prevent and/or resolve transfer pricing disputes?

Available mechanisms: Rulings; Mutual Agreement Procedures.

Tax rulings are available to provide a transfer pricing position (not a unilateral APA) on related party transactions. The MAP procedure is also available for taxpayers and MAP guidance is provided for on the website of the UAE Ministry of Finance.

Further information is available at the UAE's OECD MAP Profile.

Sources: [UAE's MAP Profile](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-sub-issues/dispute-resolution/united-arab-emirates-dispute-resolution-profile.pdf)

## Safe Harbours

### Q39. Does your jurisdiction provide for any safe harbours or other simplification measures in respect of certain industries, types of taxpayers, or types of transactions?

**Yes.**

Whilst not directly related to transfer pricing, the UAE has a Small Business Relief regime whereby companies with total revenues of less than AED 3 million (approx. USD 800 000) in each financial year prior to 2027 can benefit from Small Business Relief where a simplified tax return is required and no taxable income is deemed to arise. Such entities are also exempt from transfer pricing documentation requirements.

## Attribution of Profits to Permanent Establishments

### Q43. Which version of Article 7 of the OECD Model Tax Convention on Income and on Capital do your tax treaties contain?

The UAE has a large tax treaty network which contain treaties signed across the past thirty years and, as such, there are a variety of Article 7 provisions across these treaties, some of which follow the OECD Model and some follow the UN Model.

### Q44. For tax treaties containing Article 7 as it read before 2010, does your jurisdiction apply the authorized OECD approach?

The AOA is not adopted in domestic corporate tax law and thus the approach to profit attribution of permanent establishments would be reliant on the relevant tax treaty provisions.

### Q45. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework contain specific guidance for the attribution of profits to permanent establishments of non resident entities?

**No.**
