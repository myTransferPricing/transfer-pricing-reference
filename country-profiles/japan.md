# Japan transfer pricing rules

Transfer pricing rules in Japan, summarised from the OECD Transfer Pricing Country Profile for Japan (updated October 2025). The profile is the questionnaire the Japan tax administration completed for the OECD, covering the arm's length principle, transfer pricing methods, comparability, intangibles, intra-group services, financial transactions, documentation, dispute resolution and safe harbours. This page holds all 44 answered questions in plain markdown.

## The Arm's Length Principle

### Q1. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework make reference to the arm's length principle?

**Yes.**

Sources: [Para 1 Article 66-4 of Act on Special Measures concerning Taxation ("ASMT")](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4)

### Q2. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework give the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines any role or status?

**Yes.**

The Commissioner's Directive on the Operation of Transfer Pricing ("CDOTP") prescribes that the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines shall be referred to in the course of examination or APA.

Sources: [1-2(3) of Commissioner's Directive on the Operation of Transfer Pricing ("CDOTP").](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/01.htm)

### Q3. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for a definition of related parties applicable for transfer pricing purposes?

**Yes.**

" A foreign-related party"is defined as a foreign corporation having an"associated relationship" with another corporation.

A corporation is treated as "associated" with the other if either of them holds 50 percent or more of the total number of issued stocks or the amount of investment of the other corporation.

The Order for Enforcement of ASMT prescribes the other cases to be treated as having an "associated relationship".

Sources: [Para 1 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4); [Para 1 to 4 Article 39-12 of Order for Enforcement of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332CO0000000043_20201001_502CO0000000286&keyword=%E7%A7%9F%E7%A8%8E%E7%89%B9%E5%88%A5%E6%8E%AA%E7%BD%AE%E6%B3%95%E6%96%BD%E8%A1%8C%E4%BB%A4)

## Transfer Pricing Methods

### Q4. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for transfer pricing methods to be used in respect of transactions between related parties?

**Yes.**

Methods provided for: CUP, Resale Price, Cost Plus, TNMM, Profit Split, Other.

In respect of "Other", the Order for Enforcement of ASMT provides for the valuation technique based on projected cash flows.

The Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT and the CDOTP provide guidance on the selection of the most appropriate method, which is basically consistent with the TPG.

Sources: [Para. 2 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4); [Para. 6 to 8 Article 39-12](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332CO0000000043_20201001_502CO0000000286&keyword=%E7%A7%9F%E7%A8%8E%E7%89%B9%E5%88%A5%E6%8E%AA%E7%BD%AE%E6%B3%95%E6%96%BD%E8%A1%8C%E4%BB%A4) of Order for Enforcement of ASMT [Chapter 4-1, CDOTP](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/03.htm); [66-4(2) - 1 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4i.htm); [66-4(3) - 3 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4b-00.htm)

### Q5. Which criterion is provided for in your domestic transfer pricing framework for the application of transfer pricing methods?

Most appropriate method.

From the above methods, the most appropriate method should be selected, considering the facts and circumstances of each controlled transaction, including functions performed and risks assumed.

Sources: [Para. 2 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4)

### Q6. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework contain specific guidance on commodity transactions?

**Yes.**

For controlled transactions involving commodities, the guidance contained in paragraphs 2.18-2.22 of the TPG is followed.

Sources: [Para 2 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4)

## Comparability Analysis

### Q7. Does your jurisdiction follow the guidance on comparability analysis outlined in Chapter III of the TPG?

**Yes.**

The Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT is basically consistent with the TPG.

Sources: [66-4(3) - 3 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4b-00.htm)

### Q8. Is there a preference in your jurisdiction for domestic comparables over foreign comparables?

**Yes.**

The Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT prescribes that market conditions shall be taken into consideration in performing the comparability analysis.

Sources: [66-4(3) - 3 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4b-00.htm)

### Q9. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework permit the use of secret comparables for transfer pricing assessment purposes?

**Yes.**

If the Local File is not presented or submitted by a certain appointed date, the tax authorities can inspect persons engaged in similar businesses by asking questions.

Information obtained through such inspections may be used to determine the arm's length price.

Sources: [Para. 17, 18 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4)

### Q10. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework allow or require the use of an arm's length range and/or statistical measure for determining arm's length remuneration?

**Yes.**

The Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT provides that no correction shall be made if the arm's length price of the transaction to be verified is within the range of several comparable transactions.

Sources: [66-4(3) - 4 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4b-00.htm)

### Q11. Are comparability adjustments required under your domestic transfer pricing framework?

**Yes.**

The CDOTP prescribes differences such as trade terms and settlement terms as a case where comparability adjustment is required.

Sources: [Para. 2 Article 66-4 of ASMT.](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4); [Para. 6 to 8 Article 39-12](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332CO0000000043_20201001_502CO0000000286&keyword=%E7%A7%9F%E7%A8%8E%E7%89%B9%E5%88%A5%E6%8E%AA%E7%BD%AE%E6%B3%95%E6%96%BD%E8%A1%8C%E4%BB%A4) of Order for Enforcement of ASMT [Chapter 4-4, CDOTP](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/03.htm)

## Intangible Property

### Q12. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework contain guidance specific to the pricing of controlled transactions involving intangibles?

**Yes.**

The domestic transfer pricing framework follows the guidance in Chapter VI of the TPG.

The Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT and the CDOTP provide guidance on transactions involving intangibles addressing issues such as activities that contribute to the value of the intangibles, ownership of intangible property and licensing transactions.

Sources: [66-4 (8) - 2 and 7 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4e.htm); [Chapter 3-12 to 3-14, CDOTP](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/02.htm)

### Q13. Are there any other rules outside your transfer pricing framework that are relevant for the pricing of controlled transactions involving intangibles?

**No.**

## Hard-to-Value Intangibles

### Q14. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework contain guidance specific to hard-to-value intangibles (HTVI)?

**Yes.**

The domestic transfer pricing framework follows the guidance on HTVI in Chapter VI of the TPG.

Basically, the approach as a whole is designed based on the guidance included in the TPG.

Sources: [Para. 8 to 10 Article 66-4 of ASMT.](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4); [66-4 (9) - 1 to 4 of Commis s ioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4l.htm); [Chapter 3-6](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/02.htm) and [4-15, CDOTP](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/03.htm)

### Q15. If your jurisdiction applies the HTVI approach, what are the conditions for the application of the HTVI approach?

Briefly, the HTVI approach would be applied to the transactions of specific intangibles, and the main conditions are summarized as follows (*).

- The intangibles are unique and valuable (therefore, there would be no reliable comparables).

- The intangibles are considered to be priced based on the projection of future income (or cash flows) expected to be derived from the transferred intangibles.

- The assumptions used in pricing the intangibles (including the above projection of future income or cash flows) are considered highly uncertain.

(*) There are also some exemptions for the transactions to which the HTVI approach would not be applied. Basically, the approach as a whole is designed based on the guidance included in the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines.

Sources: [Para. 8 to 10 Article 66-4 of ASMT.](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4); [66-4 (9) - 1 to 4 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4l.htm); [Para. 14 to 1 8 Article 39-12 of Order for Enforcement of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332CO0000000043_20201001_502CO0000000286&keyword=%E7%A7%9F%E7%A8%8E%E7%89%B9%E5%88%A5%E6%8E%AA%E7%BD%AE%E6%B3%95%E6%96%BD%E8%A1%8C%E4%BB%A4)

### Q16. Are transactions falling within the scope of the HTVI approach subject to a transfer pricing analysis different from the one established in Chapters I and VI?

In general, the transactions falling within the scope of the HTVI approach would be subject to the transfer pricing analysis same as for other transactions for transfer pricing purpose.

### Q17. What is the statute of limitations applicable to transactions falling within the scope of the HTVI approach in your domestic transfer pricing framework?

In general, for the corporate income tax, the statute of limitations for transfer pricing purpose is 6 years (7 years for the business years commencing on or after 1st April 2020). This is not relevant whether the transactions are falling within the scope of the HTVI approach.

Sources: [Para. 27 Article 66-4 of ASMT.](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_4)

### Q18. Can taxpayers request a bilateral or multilateral advance pricing agreement for transactions falling within the scope of the HTVI approach?

**Yes.**

APA procedures in Japan are prescribed in the Administrative Guidelines (CDOTP and Commissioner's Directive on the Mutual Agreement Procedure). The Administrative Guidelines allow a taxpayer to request a bilateral or a multilateral APA for a transaction falling within the scope of HTVI approach as well as other types of transactions.

Domestic legislation prevents the use of hindsight for making adjustments in the

Sources: [Chapter 3-6](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/02.htm) and [4-15, CDOTP](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/03.htm)

### Q20. Is it possible for your tax administration to make adjustments under the HTVI approach in open years amounts pertaining to closed years?

**No.**

### Q21. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework allow the tax administration to make corresponding adjustments under the HTVI approach in open years for amounts pertaining to closed years?

**N/A.**

In Japan, such approach as described in this question is not necessary because there is de facto no time limit for implementing MAP agreements entailing downward adjustments to be made by Japan under Japan's tax law.

### Q22. Is it possible for your tax administration to make several adjustments for one single HTVI transaction under the HTVI approach?

**N/A.**

There are no specific provisions to the HTVI approach regarding this question.

## Intra-group Services

### Q23. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide guidance specific to intra-group services transactions?

**Yes.**

The domestic transfer pricing framework follows the guidance in Chapter VII of the TPG.

The Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT and the CDOTP provide guidance on IGS addressing issues such as the relationships between services and intangibles, benefit tests and the TPMs to be applied.

Sources: [66-4 (8) - 6 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4e.htm); [Chapter 3-9 to 3-11, CDOTP](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/jimu-unei/hojin/010601/02.htm)

### Q24. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for or allow the application of a simplified approach for low value-adding intra-group services?

**Yes.**

The domestic transfer pricing framework follows the low value-adding services approach in Chapter VII.

The CDOTP provides guidance on the simplified approach for low value-adding intra-group services, which is basically consistent with the TPG.

### Q25. Are there any other rules outside your transfer pricing framework for pricing intragroup services?

**No.**

## Transfer Pricing Documentation

### Q29. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework require the taxpayer to prepare transfer pricing documentation?

**Yes.**

Required documentation: Master file consistent with Annex I to Chapter V of the TPG; Local file consistent with Annex II to Chapter V of the TPG; Country-by-country report consistent with Annex III to Chapter V of the TPG.

Sources: [Master file Para. 1 Article 66-4 - 5 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf); [Local file Para. 6 Article 66-4 of ASMT CbCR](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf); [Para. 1 Article 66-4 - 4 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf)

### Q30. Please briefly explain the relevant requirements related to each transfer pricing documentation requirement.

Master file (Submitted in Japanese or English)

To be submitted to the competent District Director within one year of the day following the one when the Ultimate Parent Entity's fiscal year ends

Local file (no language specific requirement)

Deadline for preparation: To be prepared by final return filing due to date, and to be maintained for seven years.

Deadline for submission: To be presented or submitted within a certain appointed period if requested during the course of a tax examination.

CbCR (in English)

Deadline for submission: To be submitted to the competent District Director within one year of the day following the one when the Ultimate Parent Entity's fiscal year ends.

Deadline for notification: To be notified to the competent District Director no later than the last day of the Ultimate Parent Entity's fiscal year.

Sources: Master file [Para. 1 Article 66-4 - 5](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf) of ASMT. [Article 22-10-5](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332M50000040015_20210101_502M60000040021) of Ordinance for Enforcement of ASMT; Local file [Para. 6, 12 to 15 Article 66-4](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf) of ASMT [Para 6 to 8, 11, 12 Article 22-10](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332M50000040015_20210101_502M60000040021) of Ordinance for Enforcement of ASMT; CbCR [Para. 1, 5 Article 66-4 - 4](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf) of ASMT. [Article 22-10-4](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332M50000040015_20210101_502M60000040021) of Ordinance for Enforcement of ASMT

### Q31. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for specific transfer pricing penalties and/or compliance incentives?

**Yes.**

Master file / CbCR-Fine of up to 300,000 yen if corporations fail to submit a Master file/CbC Report to the District Director by the deadline without good reason

Local file-For ensuring the duty of transfer pricing documentation, if the local file is not presented or submitted by a certain appointed date, the tax authorities can impose tax by estimation.

Sources: [Master file Para. 3 Article 66-4 - 5 of ASMT CbCR Para. 7 Article 66-4 - 4 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf); [Local file Para. 12, 14 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf)

### Q32. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework provide for exemption from transfer pricing documentation obligations?

**Yes.**

Master file / CbCR-MNE Groups with total consolidated revenue for the Ultimate Parent Entity's preceding fiscal year of less than 100 billion yen

Local file-Corporations are exempted from the duty of contemporaneous documentation for Controlled Transactions with one foreign-related party during the current business year, if:

(1) the amount of transactions (total of receipts and payments) with the foreign-related party during the previous business year (the current business year if there was not the previous one) was less than five billion yen, and (2) the amount of transactions of intangibles (total of receipts and payments) with the foreign-related party during the previous business year (the current business year if there was not the previous one) was less than 300 million yen.

Sources: [Master file Para. 1 Article 66-4 - 5 of ASMT Para. 4 Article 66-4 - 4 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf); [Local file Para. 7 Article 66-4 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf); [CbCR Para. 1 Article 66-4 - 4 of ASMT Para. 4 Article 66-4 - 4 of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/h28iten-kakaku_en.pdf)

## Dispute Avoidance and Resolution

### Q33. Which mechanisms are available in your jurisdiction to prevent and/or resolve transfer pricing disputes?

Available mechanisms: Advance Pricing Agreements (APA): Unilateral APAs, Bilateral APAs, Multilateral APAs; International Compliance Assurance Programme (ICAP); Mutual Agreement Procedures; Other: In addition to the mechanisms above, judicial process is also permitted for resolving the transfer pricing disputes..

Japan has in place a bilateral APA programme that enables taxpayers to request rollbacks. Japan publishes clear rules, guidelines and procedures on access to and use of the MAPs.

All the relevant information is available on the published Japan's MAP profile as well as in the elements B.8 – B.10 of Japan's Peer review report (Stage 2).

Sources: [Japan's MAP Profile](https://www.oecd.org/tax/dispute/Japan-Dispute-Resolution-Profile.pdf)

### Q34. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework allow the application of the simplified and streamlined approach for baseline marketing and distribution activities?

**No.**

Currently, there are no guidelines regarding the simplified and streamlined approach for baseline marketing and distribution activities. However, we published a brief guidance on practical issues on 30 June, 2025.

Sources: [Frequently Asked Questions on the Simplified and Streamlined Approach (June 2025)](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/kokusai/kansokagourika/index.htm)

### Q37. Does your jurisdiction respect the outcome of the application of the simplified and streamlined approach by a covered jurisdiction?

**Yes.**

## Attribution of Profits to Permanent Establishments

### Q43. Which version of Article 7 of the OECD Model Tax Convention on Income and on Capital do your tax treaties contain?

Article 7 as it read before 2010 and Article 7 as it reads after 2010.

In 60 tax treaties as of 1 June 2025.

In 14 tax treaties as of 1 June 2025.

### Q44. For tax treaties containing Article 7 as it read before 2010, does your jurisdiction apply the authorized OECD approach?

**Yes.**

Japan's domestic law is consistent with the AOA. Where tax treaties contain Article 7 as it read before 2010, the domestic law is subject to the provisions of those treaties.

Sources: [Article 142 of Corporate Tax Act](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/ja/laws/view/3115/en#je_pt3ch2sc1at2)

### Q45. Does your domestic transfer pricing framework contain specific guidance for the attribution of profits to permanent establishments of non resident entities?

**Yes.**

They follow the AOA as described in the 2010 Report on the Attribution of Profits to Permanent Establishments.

Corporation Tax Act and the ASMT provides definition of permanent establishments of non-resident entities and rules related to transfer pricing that are in line with AOA in the 2010 Report.

In addition, the Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT and Commissioner's Directive on the Operation of AOA provide guidance for the attribution of profits to permanent establishments, which is basically consistent with the AOA in the 2010 Report.

Sources: [Articles 66-4 - 3 of ASMT](https://elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=332AC0000000026#Mp-At_66_5); [66-4 - 3(1) - 1 to (10) - 2 of Commissioner's Directive on Interpretation of ASMT](https://www.nta.go.jp/law/tsutatsu/kobetsu/hojin/sochiho/750214/12/12_66_4l.htm); Commissioner's Directive on the Operation of AOA
