Everything in this repository is general information, not tax advice. Check the current OECD text and national law before relying on any of it. This page records the legal basis on which the material is published, and how it was checked.

## OECD licence: what we checked and what we found

Checked on 28 September 2026.

1. **The OECD's default licence is Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0).** The [OECD Open Access Policy](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/oecd-open-by-default-policy.html) applies it to OECD written content published from 1 July 2024, including publications, working papers, policy papers and country notes. The OECD announced the change in a [press release of July 2024](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/press-releases/2024/07/oecd-data-publications-and-analysis-become-freely-accessible.html). The [OECD Terms and Conditions](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/terms-conditions.html) set out the attribution rules.
2. **The country profiles used here all post-date the policy.** Every profile in `country-profiles/` was updated by the OECD between July 2025 and January 2026, so the CC BY 4.0 licence applies to them directly. The date is recorded in each file's front matter.
3. **The profile PDFs carry no separate restriction.** Each profile's footnote states that the information is provided directly by the jurisdiction, that the OECD does not certify its accuracy, and that the profile is for information purposes only and is not to be used in substitution for the jurisdiction's legal instruments, jurisprudence or administrative guidance. We repeat that caveat in every file.
4. **Third-party material.** The OECD terms note that OECD content may exceptionally include material owned by third parties, for which the user must obtain permission. The country profiles quote national legislation and administrative guidance, which the jurisdictions supplied for publication. We link to the official sources the jurisdictions cited rather than reproducing them beyond what the profile itself contains.

## How we comply

The OECD terms require attribution, and for adapted works an additional statement. Every file derived from OECD material therefore carries:

- **Attribution**: the name of the OECD work, its date, and a link to it on oecd.org.
- **Adaptation statement**, in the OECD's own wording: "This is an adaptation of an original work by the OECD. The opinions expressed and arguments employed in this adaptation should not be reported as representing the official views of the OECD or of its Member countries."
- **Accuracy caveat**: the information is provided by the jurisdiction, the OECD does not certify it, and it is not a substitute for the law.

Our summaries are adaptations, not verbatim copies: we restructure the questionnaire into markdown, shorten question wording, and add links, an introduction and related material. The OECD did not review or endorse them.
